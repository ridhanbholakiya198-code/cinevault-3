Cookies & Local Storage

Effective · Version 1.0 · Operated by the Parda developer

In short: no advertising cookies, no trackers, no analytics. We only keep what the app needs to work on your device.

Parda does not use advertising or analytics cookies. We use browser storage the app needs, so no cookie banner is shown. If that changes, we will ask you first.

What is stored on your device

  • cv_list — your watch list (statuses, favourites, alerts). Removed when you sign out or clear site data.
  • cv_taste — on-device personalisation (languages and genres you open or search). Never uploaded. Reset it from My list.
  • cv_reg — the streaming region you picked for “Where to watch”.
  • c:…, hid:…, hbd:… — short-lived caches of film data and images so pages load faster.
  • cv_addons_guest — add-on links you added without an account, kept on this device only. cv_addons_off — which add-ons you switched off.
  • cv_pl_fit, cv_wt_shape — your player picture preferences.
  • cv_key — only if you entered your own TMDB key in the app.
  • cv_u — remembers that you are signed in (name and photo) so your session restores quickly. cv_nag — you dismissed the optional sign-in suggestion.
  • cv_i, cv_np (session only) — intro animation and prompt already shown this visit.
  • Firebase Authentication — keeps your sign-in session in the browser’s IndexedDB until you sign out.

Third parties that may set their own storage

When you tap “Play trailer”, a YouTube player (privacy-enhanced mode) loads and Google/YouTube may set cookies; nothing from YouTube loads before you tap play. If you play a torrent-type stream in the browser, the Webtor player and its script host (jsDelivr) load at that moment and may set their own storage.

Your control

Clear site data in your browser at any time, or sign out from the Account menu to remove your list from the device. Clearing site data also removes add-ons saved without an account. See also the Privacy Policy.
