Privacy Policy

This policy explains what CineVault collects and why. It is effective from 23 October 2026.

CineVault doesn’t keep a history of what you watch. Browsing without an account collects no personal account data.

1. What we collect

Account: your email address and sign-in details are handled by Firebase Authentication. We never see your password.

Add-ons: the add-on URLs you save are stored in your account in Cloud Firestore, and only you can read them. Some add-on URLs contain personal settings, so treat them as private.

Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and requested page, for security and reliability.

2. Third parties

Your browser talks to services outside CineVault: the add-ons you add, video players such as YouTube or Webtor, and Firebase. They receive your IP address and the request they are asked to handle, such as a title ID, and they follow their own privacy policies.

Check what an add-on does before you add it.

3. How we use data

We use your data to sign you in and to load the add-ons you saved. We don’t sell it.

4. Your choices

You can remove any saved add-on from the add-on list at any time. To access, export or delete your account and its data, contact us and we will handle the request.

5. Changes

We may update this policy. The version shown on this page is the current one.
