Privacy Policy

Effective · Version 1.0 · Operated by the Parda developer

In short: no ads, no analytics, no selling data. Guests are not profiled. If you make an account we store your email, your list and your add-on links. Add-on requests go to the add-on you chose, and may be relayed through our server.

1. About this policy

This explains what data Parda handles, why, and your choices. We follow India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and, for visitors in the EU/UK, apply GDPR-style rights. the Parda developer is the data fiduciary / controller. This policy is also available at /privacy.html.

2. What we collect

Browsing as a guest: we do not create a profile of you. Your list, personalisation, region and any add-ons you add are stored only on your device. Our hosting provider logs standard technical data (IP address, browser, pages requested) to deliver and secure the site.

With an account:

  • Firebase Authentication holds your email, display name, profile photo link (if you use Google), sign-in method and a unique user ID. Passwords are handled by Firebase; we never see them.
  • Our database holds your list entries (title ID, type, title, poster path, language, genre IDs, status, favourite and alert flags, last-updated time), the add-on links you saved, and the version and time you accepted these Terms and this Policy.

We do not collect: payment details, GPS location, contacts, advertising IDs, or analytics. We show no third-party ads and we do not sell personal data.

3. Add-ons and the relay

  • Parda includes no add-ons. If you add one, its address is saved (on your device, or in your account if signed in). When you open a film, your browser asks that add-on, not Parda, for links, sending the film’s IMDb ID. The add-on’s operator can see your IP address and that request, under its own policy.
  • If an add-on does not allow direct browser access, Parda retries the request through our server function (/api/proxy). It only passes add-on information (manifest, catalogue and link lists) through; it does not store it, and we do not keep our own log of it.
  • Important: add-on addresses can contain private settings or access keys. When relayed, the address passes through our hosting provider (Vercel), which may keep standard request logs for a limited time. Do not add links you are not comfortable sending.
  • Video itself is never relayed through Parda; it loads from the source directly.

4. Playback and third-party players

Trailers play from YouTube. Direct links play in your browser straight from their host, which sees your IP address. If you play a torrent-type link in the browser, its magnet/info-hash and your IP are sent to Webtor (and its script is loaded from jsDelivr). If you open a link in another app (VLC, a torrent app), that app handles it under its own policy.

5. Personalisation

Recommendations such as “Picked for you” are computed on your device from titles you open or search. This profile is never uploaded. Reset it from My list.

6. Why we use data, and our basis

To provide the Service you asked for (sign-in, saving and syncing your list, loading add-on links), to keep it secure and prevent abuse, and to meet legal duties. Account data is processed on your consent, which you can withdraw by deleting your account.

7. Who processes data

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore) — account and list data.
  • Vercel — hosts the site and server functions (film data, images, add-on relay).
  • TMDB — film data and images. Requests normally pass through our server, so TMDB does not see your IP; if that route fails your browser may contact TMDB directly.
  • YouTube (Google) — only after you tap “Play trailer”.
  • JustWatch — streaming availability, via TMDB; its links open on their site.
  • Google Fonts — fonts load from Google, which receives your IP address and browser details.
  • Wikidata (Wikimedia) — for some franchise and person details your browser may query Wikidata.
  • Webtor and jsDelivr — only when you play a torrent-type link in the browser.
  • Add-on operators — whoever runs an add-on you choose.

We share data with authorities only where the law requires it.

8. Transfers outside India

These providers operate globally, so your data may be processed outside India under their own security and legal safeguards.

9. How long we keep it

Account data is kept until you delete your account. Deleting it from the Account menu removes your list, saved add-ons and account; copies in routine backups and provider logs expire on their normal schedules. Guest data stays on your device until you clear it.

10. Security

Connections use HTTPS and database rules allow only you to read or change your own data. No online service is perfectly secure, so use a strong, unique password. If a breach affects your data we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

11. Your rights

You may access, correct, export and erase your data, withdraw consent, nominate someone to act for you, and complain about how your data is handled. Export and Delete are in the Account menu. For anything else write to the contact address shown in the app; we aim to reply within 30 days. You may also approach the Data Protection Board of India or your local regulator.

12. Children

Accounts are for people 18 and over. Parda is not directed at children and we do not knowingly keep accounts of minors; if we learn of one we will delete it.

13. Changes

If we change this Policy we will update the version and date above and tell you in the app when the change is material.

14. Contact and grievance officer

the Parda developer — the contact address shown in the app. Complaints about data handling can be sent to the same address.
