About

PARDA

Explore films in their own colour.

A cinematic way to discover films, series and anime, keep track of what you watch, and see where to stream it.

The idea

Every film has a colour.

Parda lets that colour take over the screen: the glow, the mood, the whole interface. Browsing should feel like walking into a cinema, not scrolling a spreadsheet.

What it does

Three things, done properly.

Discover

Films, series and anime across genres, languages and eras, with a fresh hero every visit.

Keep track

Build your list and mark what you are watching, finished or planning. Sign in to keep it with you.

Know where to watch

See which services carry a title in your region before you go looking.

Straight talk

Parda hosts nothing.

Parda does not host, store or upload any video. Any playable links come from third-party sources, and their content is not controlled by Parda.

Titles, posters, artwork and trailers belong to their owners. If you believe something reachable through Parda infringes your rights, write to us and read the Terms.
